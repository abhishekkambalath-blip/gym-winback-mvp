# FAQ

## Can I switch branches?
Yes. Members can use any of our three branches (Koramangala, Indiranagar, HSR Layout) at no extra cost. Just show the membership QR at the front desk. A permanent home-branch change takes one day to update.

## What are the opening hours?
All branches are open 5:30 AM to 10:30 PM on weekdays and 6:30 AM to 9:00 PM on weekends and public holidays.

## How do I book a class?
Open the gym app, pick the class and time slot, and tap Book. Bookings open 48 hours ahead and can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the class. Walk-ins are welcome if there is space.

## How do I update my card or fix a failed payment?
Open the app, go to Membership, then Payment method, and add the new card. The failed payment retries automatically within 24 hours. The front desk can also help. If a payment has failed, staff can give a 7-day grace period.

## Are there classes for beginners?
Yes. Every branch runs beginner-friendly Yoga, Zumba and Spin. Indiranagar has a Saturday 8 AM beginner Yoga flow and HSR Layout starts a CrossFit beginners' batch every first Monday. A free PT session is a good first step for anyone who feels unsure.

## Can I pause my membership?
Yes. A free pause of up to 30 days is available for travel, injury or a busy period. Ask the front desk or reply to us and we will set it up. The contract end date moves by the same number of days.
