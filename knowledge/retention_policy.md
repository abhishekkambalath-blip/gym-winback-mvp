# Retention policy

Applies to all three branches (Koramangala, Indiranagar, HSR Layout). Staff review every win-back message before it is sent.

## What staff may offer
Staff MAY offer any of these, without asking a manager:
- A free 30-minute personal training (PT) session.
- A free membership pause of up to 30 days.
- A 7-day grace period on a failed or late payment.
- One guest pass per month, so the member can bring a friend.

## What staff may not offer
Staff may NOT offer any of these:
- Discounts over 10%.
- Refunds.
- Free months.
- Cancellation-fee waivers.
Never promise any of these, even if the member pushes. Say a manager will look into it and move on.

## If a member asks to cancel
Never argue and never guilt the member. Acknowledge the request kindly, then offer a pause (up to 30 days, free) or a free PT session first. Only if the member still wants to cancel, point them to the front desk. Do not mention the cancellation fee unless the member asks.

## Failed payments
Keep the tone helpful, never accusatory. Assume it is a card or bank hiccup, not a choice. Offer the 7-day payment grace period and explain how to update the card (see the FAQ). Do not mention amounts owed in a message.

## New members (first 90 days)
Members in their first 90 days are the highest priority. The habit is still forming, so a personal touch matters most: a free PT session to build a plan, or a class time that fits their routine.

## Tone
Warm, human, like a coach who noticed they were missed. No pressure, no guilt, no talk of "tracking" members or losing progress.
