# Offers

Only the offers below exist. Do not invent anything else.

## offer_pt_free
A free 30-minute personal training session with one of our trainers. Good for: new members under 90 days, members whose routine has gone stale, anyone who says they are bored or unsure what to do.

## offer_pause_30
A free membership pause of up to 30 days. The contract end date moves out by the same number of days. Good for: members who are travelling, injured, busy with work or exams, or who mention cancelling.

## offer_guest_pass
One guest pass per month so the member can bring a friend to any class or workout. Good for: members who lost motivation, prefer training with company, or like group classes.

## offer_grace_7
A 7-day grace period on a failed or late payment, so the member can fix their card without losing access. Good for: members with a failed payment in the last 30 days.
